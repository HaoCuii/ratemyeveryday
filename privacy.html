Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version

You can use ratemyeveryday without an account; your ratings then stay in your browser and never reach us. If you sign in, we store your ratings so you can see them anywhere and share them with friends you accept. We don't sell data, show ads, or use trackers.

Without signing in

Your ratings, notes and display preferences are saved only in your browser's local storage on that device. We can't see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

When you sign in with Google

Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We also store what you create:

  • the handle you choose, your name and profile picture (so friends can recognise you)
  • your daily ratings (1 to 10) and the notes you attach
  • your friend requests and friendships

If you rated days before signing in, they are copied from your browser into your account when you sign in.

Who can see it

  • Your ratings and notes: you, and people whose friend request you accepted (or who accepted yours). Pending requests show nothing.
  • Your handle, name and profile picture: other signed-in people, so they can find you to send a request.
  • Your email address: no other users.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) in the United States. Sign-in is provided by Google. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can clear any day's rating at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email haocui43@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Contact
